# Roadmap

One roadmap across everything here, rather than a separate one per repo. It covers three things: [Practical AI Sales Workflows](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/practical-ai-sales-workflows), the [Sibling Projects](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/sibling-projects) family of eighteen generalised tools, and the newer commercial-teams family ([AI for Commercial Teams](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/ai-for-commercial-teams), [Sales Conversation Gym](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/sales-conversation-gym), [Sales Proof Bench](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/sales-proof-bench), [Sales Value Workshop](https://github.com/shaunmarsden/sales-value-workshop)).

Each repo keeps its own detailed roadmap where one exists. This page is the short version: what is actually active, what is next, and what is genuinely parked rather than forgotten.

## Now

- **Three logged real-work tests still needed** for Practical AI Sales Workflows: handing over an opportunity, moving a stalled decision, and reviewing an outbound campaign. The other jobs already have one.
- **The feedback form and Discussions link on the sales repo have not been used by anyone yet.** That is the realistic way independent evidence arrives; log whatever comes in, even a single line.
- **Sales Proof Bench owes two more comparison types**, per its own roadmap: a setup comparison (cold versus a carefully scoped instruction) and a workflow comparison (one-off prompting versus a repeatable checklist). Right now it only has a model comparison.
- **The commercial-teams family keeps growing.** New scenarios, cases and tools are fair game there without waiting for a fixed evidence bar first, though each repo keeps its own stated guardrail about which specific features wait for real team use (a dashboard, a leaderboard, a calculator) before being added.

## Next

- More scenarios for Sales Conversation Gym: a business case conversation with a finance stakeholder, a sales-to-customer-success handover, a lost opportunity review where the stated reason may not be the real one.
- More cases for Sales Value Workshop: a mid-market customer with a clear pain but no agreed owner, an enterprise customer where procurement is the main gate, an existing customer improving a process without buying anything new.
- More cases for Sales Proof Bench: pre-call preparation, objection diagnosis with ambiguous wording, business case drafting with missing baseline evidence.
- Fix repeated navigation or wording problems on the sales repo when real use actually surfaces one, rather than guessing at them.

## Later, Deliberately Parked

These are not forgotten, they are genuinely on hold, most waiting on something specific rather than just priority.

- **Sibling repos for finance, HR or operations.** The plan was always to find a genuine practitioner from that function to co-drive it, not build it solo by analogy the way the sales repo works because it is tested against real deals. No practitioner has been found. Not being actively pursued until one is.
- **BVA and workshop-style enterprise content.** This reads as AiCore-flavoured methodology rather than generic, small-business-friendly advice. May need to stay private, or get a proper generalisation pass, before it is worth publishing.
- **A proper visuals and short-video pass** for the sales repo, beyond the diagrams already in place. Flagged before as needing a genuinely concentrated session rather than a few minutes here and there.
- **Better, more natural voices for the interactive demo.** Blocked on either generating the audio files directly or sharing an API key with billing attached; the current browser voice reads as dated.
- **An n8n worked example** inside the sales repo's own automation guide, as one honest example of a repeatable, approval-gated workflow, not a new headline tool.

## A Few Rules for Using This Page

- Keep facts, estimates and assumptions clearly separate, including on this page: nothing here is a delivery date.
- Add to "Later" freely. Only move something to "Next" when it is actually being worked on.
- If a "Later" item stays parked for good, say so plainly and remove it, rather than let this page collect ideas nobody intends to build.
